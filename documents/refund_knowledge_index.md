## Key Information Categories

### Technical Information

- **Refund Types**: Normal, Instant, Batch
- **Refund States**: Created, Processed, Failed
- **Reference Numbers**: RRN, ARN, UTR
- **Auto-Refund Scenarios**: Closed QR Code, Non-Auto-Captured Payments

### Business Policies

- **Fees and Charges**: No fees for normal refunds, small fee for instant refunds
- **Refund Limitations**: Time, amount, and irrevocable nature
- **Source Refunds**: Money refunded to original payment method
- **Account Balance Requirements**: Sufficient balance needed for processing refunds

### Customer Communication

- **Tracking Information**: Providing reference numbers for bank tracking
- **Setting Expectations**: 5-7 working days for bank crediting
- **Bank Dispute Guidance**: Steps to take if bank doesn't recognize transaction
- **Multiple Refunds Handling**: Explanation and tracking for each refund

### User Data Requirements

- **Refund Identification**: Refund ID, Payment ID, Transaction date
- **Payment Details**: Amount, method, currency
- **Reference Numbers**: RRN/ARN/UTR
- **Merchant Information**: Merchant ID, Store ID, Device ID
- **Customer Information**: Bank account details, statements, contact information
